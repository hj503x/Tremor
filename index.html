The golden rules

core macro mechanics

Hot regime — strong economy + high inflation

USD ↑ · Yields ↑ · Fed more hawkish · Stocks often ↓

Cold regime — weak economy + falling inflation

USD ↓ · Yields ↓ · Fed more dovish · Stocks often ↑ (rate-cut bid)
